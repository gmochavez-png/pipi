# Surface brief: PIPI app (all screens)

Scope: the whole PWA (index.html, css/styles.css, screens rendered by js/app.js, js/chat.js, js/games.js, js/wordsearch.js). Mode: Operate (a child operates it; an adult sometimes).

Audience and job: a 6-year-old who barely reads, on iPhone and iPad equally, sometimes with an adult. She picks an activity, plays, hears PIPI, and collects stars. Constraints: PIPI 3D stays the hero of the home screen; content, audio, games and offline behavior unchanged; huge tap targets.

Pinned by the user: "new Apple style" redesign. The old look (chunky offset-shadow buttons, gray square game keys) is the anti-reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: PIPI lives on an iOS home screen. The app reads as a first-party Apple app for kids: system materials, SF Rounded, grouped insets, and refuses the toy-store look of chunky buttons with hard bottom shadows.

OWN-WORLD: iOS light system ground #F2F2F7, white grouped cards with continuous 22-28px corners and soft offset shadows, system tints (orange #FF9500, teal #30B0C7, pink #FF2D55, blue #007AFF, indigo #5856D6, yellow #FFCC00, green #34C759), SF Pro Rounded via ui-rounded, SF-Symbol-style stroked SVG icons, capsule buttons, segmented controls, translucent blurred nav bars, iMessage bubbles.

STORY: she recognises each activity by its colored app icon, taps, plays, and PIPI celebrates; adults find settings in an iOS Settings layout.

FIRST VIEWPORT: blurred top bar (sound, settings, star capsule); PIPI widget card at top with 3D PIPI and an iMessage-style speech bubble; below, a 2x2 grid of large app-icon tiles (gradient squircle with white glyph, label under) for Leer, Inglés, Cuentos, Juegos, then a full-width chat row.

FORM: pinned brief (iOS app for kids), position 1 of 1 (user-pinned direction beats the roll). seed:0353c9a661fadf7e

Signature move: the word-search marker, a rounded capsule that slides under the letters while dragging and stays tinted on found words; app-icon squircles carry it across screens.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
